Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 2026

Our commitment

SafeApp has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Our standards

  • Content, accounts, or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors is strictly prohibited on SafeApp.
  • We prohibit using SafeApp to create, store, share, request, or distribute CSAM or to groom, sextort, or otherwise harm minors.

Detection and enforcement

  • Users can report abusive content, accounts, and child safety concerns directly in the app (Report option on profiles, reels, and content) or by email at support@safeapp.live.
  • Reports are reviewed, and violating accounts are removed and permanently banned.
  • Where required, we preserve and report violations to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or equivalent bodies, and cooperate with law enforcement.

Compliance

SafeApp complies with applicable child safety laws in the regions where it operates.
