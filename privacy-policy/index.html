Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

1. Introduction

SafeApp ("SafeApp," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information and communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeApp collects, uses, stores, processes, protects, and discloses information when you access or use our applications, websites, services, messaging platform, payment features, business services, artificial intelligence features, and any other SafeApp products or services.

By using SafeApp, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Our Privacy Principles

SafeApp is built upon the following principles:

  • Privacy by Design
  • Security by Default
  • End-to-End Encryption (where available)
  • Transparency
  • User Control
  • Responsible Data Processing
  • Continuous Security Improvement
  • Compliance with Applicable Laws

These principles guide the design, operation, and ongoing development of SafeApp.

4. End-to-End Encrypted Messages

Where private conversations are protected by end-to-end encryption:

Only the intended participants are intended to read the message contents.

SafeApp is not intended to access the plaintext contents of end-to-end encrypted communications.

Encryption applies only to features specifically identified as end-to-end encrypted.

Public posts, channels, groups, cloud backups, business communications, metadata, and certain other services may not be end-to-end encrypted.

5. Information We Do NOT Sell

SafeApp does not sell users' personal information, private communications, contact lists, or personal profiles to advertisers, marketing companies, or data brokers.

SafeApp does not permit advertisers to purchase users' private conversations.

SafeApp uses administrative, technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to protect user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or destruction.

  • End-to-end encryption (where available)
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest

  • Multi-factor authentication (where available)
  • Access controls
  • Security monitoring

SafeApp believes that user trust is built through transparency.
