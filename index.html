SafeApp

A cross-platform secure messaging app built with Flutter and Firebase — offering chats, calls, stories, reels, and social feeds on web, mobile, and desktop.

SafeApp is a full-featured secure communication platform built with Flutter and Firebase. It delivers real-time one-to-one and group messaging — text, images, video, voice notes, documents, and location — plus voice and video calls via Agora, 24-hour status and stories, short-form reels, and social-style feeds, all behind phone and email OTP authentication.

Users get WhatsApp-style chat tools: replies, reactions, edits, stars, mute, pin, archive, and search. Optional app and chat locks, encrypted chat backups, push notifications, dark mode, and multi-language support come standard — with a modern UI aimed at production use on Android, iOS, web, and desktop.

Built for teams that need more

An end-to-end messaging and social product for teams and businesses that want a private, branded alternative to consumer chat apps — from a single Flutter codebase, powered by Firebase.

Rich messaging

Secure sign-in, group administration, and a full communication stack — real-time chat, media sharing, and group tools out of the box.

Calls & stories

HD voice and video calls, ephemeral 24-hour stories, camera features with AR filters, plus reels and feeds for engagement.

Security built in

Firebase rules, optional biometric app lock, passphrase-protected backups, and in-app terms and privacy policy — privacy by design.

Reels & feeds

Scroll through short-form reels and social-style feeds — discover content, stay connected, and spend your time exploring what matters to you, all inside SafeApp.
